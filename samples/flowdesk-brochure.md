# FlowDesk: HR, attendance and payroll for Indian SMEs

FlowDesk is a fictional demo product used to show the voice agent. It is cloud software that runs HR, attendance, leave and payroll for Indian companies with 10 to 1,000 employees.

## Who it is for

Small and mid-size businesses that still run payroll on Excel or an accountant, lose time on attendance disputes, or miss PF, ESI and TDS deadlines. Typical buyers are founders, HR managers and finance heads in manufacturing, retail chains, IT services, clinics and schools.

## Main features

- Payroll in one click: salary structures, arrears, reimbursements, loans and full-and-final settlement. Payslips go to employees on WhatsApp and email.
- Statutory compliance: PF, ESI, professional tax, LWF and TDS calculated automatically, with ready challans and returns. Form 16 is generated every year.
- Attendance: mobile app punch-in with GPS and selfie, biometric device integration, shift rosters and overtime rules.
- Leave management: custom leave policies, approvals on mobile, and automatic sync to payroll.
- Employee self-service app in English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Marathi.
- Reports for managers: headcount, attrition, overtime cost and payroll cost by department.

## Pricing

- Starter: ₹79 per employee per month. Payroll, compliance, leave and the employee app. Minimum 10 employees.
- Growth: ₹129 per employee per month. Everything in Starter plus attendance with GPS and biometric devices, shift rosters, and manager reports.
- Enterprise: custom price for more than 500 employees, with a dedicated account manager and custom integrations.
- Annual billing gets 2 months free (pay for 10 months, use 12).
- There is no setup fee for Starter and Growth. Data migration from Excel or another payroll tool is free.
- A 14-day free trial is available on all plans, no credit card needed.
- Prices exclude 18% GST.

## Integrations

Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Google Workspace and Microsoft 365. Biometric devices from ESSL and ZKTeco. Bank salary transfer files for HDFC, ICICI, Axis, SBI and Kotak. An open REST API is included on the Growth plan.

## Onboarding

A typical company of 50 employees goes live in 5 working days: day 1 data import, day 2 to 3 salary structures and policies, day 4 a parallel payroll run, day 5 go-live. Every customer gets a named onboarding specialist and phone support in English and Hindi, 9 am to 7 pm, Monday to Saturday.

## Security and data

Data is stored in India on AWS Mumbai, encrypted at rest and in transit. Role-based access, two-factor login and a full audit log. Daily backups are kept for 30 days. FlowDesk follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and customers can export or delete their data at any time.

## Common questions and objections

- "We already use Excel or our accountant." Excel payroll for 50 people usually takes 2 to 3 days a month and is where most compliance mistakes happen. FlowDesk runs it in under an hour and calculates PF, ESI and TDS for you.
- "It is too expensive." For 50 employees on Starter it costs ₹3,950 a month, less than one day of an HR executive's time, and late-filing penalties alone often cost more.
- "Switching is painful." Migration is free and done by our team; you run one payroll in parallel before going live.
- "We are too small." The Starter plan works from 10 employees.
- "Can we cancel?" Monthly plans can be cancelled any time with 30 days' notice. Annual plans are refundable within the first 30 days.
